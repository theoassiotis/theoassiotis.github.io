Theo Assiotis

I am a Reader (Associate Professor) in the School of Mathematics at the University of Edinburgh. Before coming to Edinburgh, I was a postdoc at Oxford and Bristol. I completed my PhD at Warwick, following undergraduate studies at Cambridge.

My pure mathematics interests are in probability theory and mathematical physics. My main contributions have been on certain aspects of the theory of random matrices and statistical mechanics.

I am also increasingly interested in the Mathematics of AI, AI safety and AI4Science broadly interpreted.
